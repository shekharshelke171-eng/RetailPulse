\# RetailPulse – AI-Powered Customer Analytics \& Demand Forecasting Platform



\## 1. Introduction



\### 1.1 Project Overview



RetailPulse is a data analytics and machine learning-based platform

developed to analyze retail sales data and generate useful business

insights.



The project focuses on sales analysis, customer segmentation,

customer churn prediction, demand forecasting, and inventory

recommendations.



The platform uses Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and Streamlit

to process retail data, build analytical models, and present results

through an interactive dashboard.



\### 1.2 Project Objectives



The main objectives of RetailPulse are:



1\. To analyze historical retail sales data.

2\. To understand customer purchasing behavior.

3\. To segment customers using the RFM approach.

4\. To identify customers who may stop purchasing.

5\. To forecast future product demand.

6\. To generate inventory recommendations.

7\. To display business insights through an interactive dashboard.



\### 1.3 Problem Statement



Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales and customer data.

Analyzing this data manually can be difficult and time-consuming.



RetailPulse aims to simplify this process by using data analysis

and machine learning techniques to identify patterns, understand

customer behavior, predict possible churn, forecast demand, and

support better inventory decisions.
